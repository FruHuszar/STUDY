# Study Notes

A personal study notebook. Public example notes are open to everyone; your own notes are private to your Google account and readable offline once loaded.

## Reading notes

- **Search** at the top finds words in every note, including code and tables.
- **Colored dots** are categories. Click one to show only that category; click it again to show everything.
- **Tags** under the note titles narrow things down further. Picking several tags shows only chapters that have all of them.
- Use the left and right arrow keys to jump between titles and chapters.

## Your private notes

1. Click **Sign in with Google**. Your account has to be on the approved list; ask the site owner to add you.
2. Click **New note**. Give it a title, a category, and a color for that category, then write in Markdown.
3. Click **Save note**. The note appears at the top of the page, marked *private*. Only you can see it.

To change a note, click **Edit** above its title. **Sync** fetches notes you changed on another device. **Sign out** removes your notes from this device.

### Writing a note

```markdown
## A chapter

#### tags
sql, school

Text, lists, tables and code blocks all work.

### A smaller chapter inside it
```

- Every `##` or `###` heading starts a chapter.
- The line after `#### tags` lists that chapter's tags, separated by commas.

### Bringing in existing Markdown files

Click **Import**, pick one or more `.md` files, and choose a category for files that don't set their own. A file's first `#` heading becomes its title. A file can set its own category at the very top:

```markdown
---
category: school
---
```

## Offline and installing

Install the site as an app from your browser's menu (or the **Install app** button). Notes you've already loaded stay readable without internet. Creating and editing notes needs a connection.
